# Keep the public profile accurate

A business profile should reflect the business's real name, locations or service area, contact details, hours, and services. Check each field against information the business has authorized you to use.

## Review the details

- Confirm that the phone number, website, address, and service area are current.
- Choose categories and services that describe the business accurately.
- Check that hours and temporary closures are up to date.
- Use current images that the business has permission to publish.
- Review public questions and customer feedback before suggesting a response.

## Avoid unsupported tactics

Do not invent reviews, offer incentives in exchange for reviews, add services the business does not provide, or claim that profile edits guarantee a ranking or click increase. Check Google's current guidance before making profile changes.

## Measure only what you can observe

Record what was changed and when. Use the business's own reporting, when authorized, to evaluate later changes. A profile inspection by itself does not establish additional calls, visits, or sales.
