## Initial post-cutover operating decision — October 4, 2026 (superseded)

The user confirmed that AWS should be shut down after cutover because ongoing monthly hosting cost is too high during development. They were told this means ProposalOS will be offline after shutdown. This is the intended post-cutover service state; no AWS shutdown or billing change has been performed. AWS has no simple billing-pause control: charges stop only as billable resources are stopped or removed, and retained data can still incur storage charges. An RDS instance can remain stopped for at most seven days and still incurs storage and backup charges; ElastiCache continues hourly charges while active and must be deleted to stop them. Account closure is a separate, high-impact action: accrued charges remain due, and AWS permanently deletes remaining resources after the 90-day post-closure period. Preserve verified backups before any shutdown. The GCP source database remains suspended and should be resumed only in the final export/cutover window to limit overlap charges.

Shutdown references: [RDS stop behavior](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/AmazonRDS/latest/UserGuide/USER_StopInstance.html), [ElastiCache charges and deletion](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/AmazonElastiCache/latest/dg/SubnetGroups.designing-cluster-pre.redis.html), and [AWS account closure](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/accounts/latest/reference/manage-acct-closing.html).
