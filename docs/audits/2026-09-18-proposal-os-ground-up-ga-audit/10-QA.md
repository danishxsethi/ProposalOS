# QA Assessment

QA is substantial but not a single release gate. `ProposalQAService` computes seven dimensions and a 7.5 average/minimum threshold. `runAutoQA` has separate weighted client-perfect thresholds and hard fails. The proposal graph has another hallucination score/retry path. Publication relies on only part of this policy.

This creates a false-confidence risk: a proposal may become READY or publishable while another QA layer would require human review or fail a client-perfect criterion. Adversarial QA unavailable due to missing credentials is not consistently represented as `QA_UNAVAILABLE`.

**State:** `PARTIALLY_IMPLEMENTED`.

**Closure:** one versioned `assertProposalPublishable()` predicate must evaluate grounding, evidence freshness, claim policy, package/pricing integrity, business/tenant identity, completeness, human review, hard fails, model/QA availability, and expiry. Persist the predicate inputs and add route-level tests proving READY/PUBLISHED/SENT/ACCEPTED cannot bypass it.
